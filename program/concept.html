Theory of Change: Multi-Track Alignment

1. Problem Grounding

Industrial Decoupling: As Pisano & Shih (2012) warn, decoupling design (thinking) from production (making) erodes national capacity. Malaysia lacks a bridge for reflective industrialization.

Normative Blindness: Western tech frameworks are often "extractive" (e.g., Swiss "Sustainable AI" obscuring Global South mining). We need contextual ijtihad to center amanah in our tech ambitions.

11. Ritual & Failure Design Meaning & Memory

Ritual Structure (Ozenc 2020)

Opening intro (20s) sets belonging. Transition silence (60s) breaks passive reception. Closing witness validates the learning community.

Productive Failure (Kapur 2008)

Struggling with the threshold dilemma before receiving input creates the knowledge gap for the input to fill.
